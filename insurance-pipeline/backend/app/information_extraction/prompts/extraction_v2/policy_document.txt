DOCUMENT TYPE: insurance policy document. Extract policy identity (insurer, product/plan/variant, policy number, UIN/product ID, wording version, issue date and subtype); explicitly named policyholder and insured members with identifiers, relationships and stated age/date of birth; coverage dates, inception/continuity and endorsement dates; sum insured, currency, basis, bonuses, additional cover and restoration/recharge terms; stated hospitalization/day-care and other benefits; pre/post-hospitalization periods; waiting periods, exclusions and exceptions; co-payment, deductible, room/ICU limits, benefit or procedure sublimits, geographic/network limits; riders, waivers, member restrictions, definitions, contractual cross-references, claim notification/submission conditions and required evidence. Preserve complete contractual clauses, conditions, exceptions, scope, units and references. Never turn conditional clauses into unconditional booleans. A wording document can be extractable without customer-specific fields. Do not infer policy-in-force, remaining balance, paid premium, eligibility or claim coverage.
